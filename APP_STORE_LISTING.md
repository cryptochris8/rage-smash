## Description
Smash. Destroy. Repeat.

Rage Smash is the ultimate stress-relief destruction game. Charge your hammer, crush objects, and rack up satisfying combos as the screen shakes, particles fly, and coins pour in.

CHARGE & SMASH
Hold to charge your hammer for maximum power. Hit the sweet spot for 2x damage — but push too far and you'll overcharge, losing your entire combo streak. Risk vs. reward, every single hit.

BUILD INSANE COMBOS
Chain smashes to build your combo multiplier. The higher you go, the more the screen shakes, the lighting shifts, and the fragments explode. Hit x20 and become UNSTOPPABLE.

JACKPOT MOMENTS
Every smash has a chance to trigger a massive jackpot — 5x to 10x your coins in a single hit. The gold confetti says it all.

HYDRAULIC PRESS BONUS
Every 10 smashes, a hydraulic press descends to crush your object for a guaranteed 3x bonus. Sit back and enjoy the destruction.

15 OBJECTS TO DESTROY
Smash soda cans, phones, trophies, rubber ducks, watermelons, and more across 3 unlockable content packs.

5 THEMED ROOMS
Destroy in the Rage Room, Office, Neon Arena, Luxury Suite, or Dungeon. Each room has its own vibe and atmosphere.

DAILY CHALLENGES
A unique 20-object sequence generated every day. Beat your personal best and climb the leaderboard.

UPGRADE YOUR HAMMER
Boost your power, speed, and combo multiplier across 3 upgrade trees. Unlock new hammer skins to smash in style.

GOALS & REWARDS
Complete session goals and daily retention challenges to earn bonus coins. Log in daily for escalating rewards up to 10,000 coins.

Free to play. No internet required. Just pure, satisfying destruction.
