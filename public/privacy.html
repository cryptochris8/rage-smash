Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 25, 2026

Rage Smash ("the Game") is a free-to-play casual game developed by Rage Smash ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

Game progress. Coins, upgrades, unlocks, and streaks are saved locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). This data never leaves your device.

Advertising data. We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data. On iOS 14.5+, you will be asked for permission before any tracking occurs (App Tracking Transparency). See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

No account required. We do not require registration, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal contact information.

2. How We Use Data

  • Save and restore your game progress on your device.
  • Show relevant ads through Google AdMob to support free gameplay.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — ad serving and mediation. Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data Retention

Game progress is stored locally and persists until you clear your browser/app data or uninstall the Game. We do not store any data on our servers.

6. Your Choices

  • On iOS, deny App Tracking Transparency to limit ad personalization.
  • On Android, opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove all locally stored progress.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

8. Contact

Questions? Reach us at privacy@ragesmash.app.
